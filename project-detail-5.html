Case Study:

Developing a Website for V8 Prod, a Photography & Videography Studio

V8 Prod is an Ottawa & Gatineau photography and videography studio (since 2022) specializing in weddings, conferences and galas, concerts, and corporate and portrait photography. I developed the website so clients can review the studio's work and enquire in a few clicks.

Challenge

The site is image-heavy, built around large photo and video galleries. It needed to showcase the work clearly — weddings, conferences/gala, concerts, corporate & brand photography, and portraits — while still loading quickly on mobile and desktop, so visitors would stay and enquire instead of dropping off.

Role

  • - Freelance Web Developer
Responsibilities & Features Implemented
  • Developed the website using WordPress, structured around its visual portfolio.
  • Organized the portfolio by service — weddings, conferences/gala, concerts, corporate & brand photography, and portraits — with a clear email/phone contact path.
  • Optimized the image-heavy site for performance and mobile, so large galleries open quickly.

Results & Technologies Used

  • - WordPress
  • - Performance Optimization
  • - Responsive Web Design

Cut load time by around 50% (about 6s to about 3s), so large galleries open quickly and more visitors enquire instead of dropping off.
